# Project Context

## Product
**VIỆT SỬ KÝ — Hành trình dựng nước và giữ nước**. A browser-based historical learning and strategy game. Primary language: Vietnamese.

## Vision
Players understand Vietnamese history through exploration, interactive learning, missions, simplified resource management, and strategic scenarios—not only by reading or answering quizzes.

## Fixed V3 choices
- Audience: students and history enthusiasts.
- Gameplay: hybrid.
- Visual: 2.5D isometric/oblique.
- Scope: chronological content beginning with Văn Lang – Âu Lạc.
- MVP: Văn Lang and Âu Lạc chapters.
- Web-first; desktop primary, tablet/mobile supported.
- React + TypeScript + Vite; Phaser 3; Java + Spring Boot; PostgreSQL.
- Modular monolith for MVP.

## Product pillars
- Learn: events, people, places, timeline, sources, quizzes and explanations.
- Play: maps, missions, exploration, building/management and scenarios.
- Discover: eras, artifacts, locations and cultural context.
- Reflect: feedback, review, progress and achievements.

## MVP includes
Authentication, profile, chapter browsing, historical content, timeline, basic map, campaign/mission flow, quiz engine, simple resource activity, progress, achievements, and editorial draft/review/publish workflow.

## Explicitly out of MVP
Real-time multiplayer, full RTS combat, large open world, native mobile apps, automated publication of AI-generated historical claims, and equal-depth coverage of all Vietnamese eras.

## Integrity
Simplified mechanics are educational/gameplay models unless supported as historically accurate. Never imply otherwise.
