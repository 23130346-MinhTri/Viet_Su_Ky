# Historical Content and Editorial Guidelines

## Content classifications
- `DOCUMENTED_FACT`: supported by reliable historical/archaeological sources.
- `INTERPRETATION`: evidence-based explanation.
- `TRADITION_OR_LEGEND`: traditional/legendary material clearly identified.
- `HISTORICAL_DEBATE`: competing interpretations or unresolved evidence.
- `GAMEPLAY_FICTION`: invented for gameplay, not asserted as history.

## Required metadata
Stable ID/code, title, summary/body, period/date label, classification, certainty note, source references, author/editor, reviewer, review status, last reviewed date and version.

## Source quality
Prefer academic research, peer-reviewed work, university/research publications, museums, archives, heritage institutions and primary sources with scholarly context. A URL alone does not prove reliability. Never fabricate author, page, quote, date or citation.

## Early periods
For Văn Lang – Âu Lạc:
- Distinguish archaeological evidence from later textual traditions.
- Avoid false precision for debated dates.
- Label legends and later narratives.
- Explain uncertainty in accessible language.

## Quiz requirements
Each question needs learning objective, prompt, options/expected answer, server-side correct answer, explanation, difficulty, topic tags, sources and review status. Do not test disputed claims as uncontested facts.

## Gameplay fiction
Resource quantities, production rates, settlement layouts and hypothetical outcomes may be simplified. Label simulation where players could mistake it for historical reconstruction.

## Workflow
`DRAFT -> IN_REVIEW -> APPROVED -> PUBLISHED`
Possible revision path: `IN_REVIEW -> REVISION_REQUIRED -> IN_REVIEW`. No unreviewed AI-generated history is published automatically.

## AI assistance
AI may summarize supplied sources, draft explanations, propose quiz questions and flag claims for verification. A human reviewer must verify evidence and approve publication. AI confidence is not historical evidence.
