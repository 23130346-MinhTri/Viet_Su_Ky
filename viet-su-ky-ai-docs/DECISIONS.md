# Product and Architecture Decision Log

## ADR-001 — Web-first
**Status:** Accepted. React is the application shell; Phaser is embedded for game scenes. This supports learning pages, accounts, content management and game interaction.

## ADR-002 — Hybrid educational gameplay
**Status:** Accepted. Combine learning, missions, exploration, simplified management and scenarios. Mechanics must support learning objectives.

## ADR-003 — 2.5D
**Status:** Accepted. Isometric/oblique presentation; scene and asset pipeline must support layers, sprites, camera and touch.

## ADR-004 — Chronological scope
**Status:** Accepted. Begin at Văn Lang – Âu Lạc and expand chronologically. MVP has two introductory chapters, not all eras at full depth.

## ADR-005 — Modular monolith
**Status:** Accepted. Spring Boot modular monolith for MVP; extract services only when justified.

## ADR-006 — Server-authoritative progression
**Status:** Accepted. Backend owns official scores, rewards, unlocks, mission completion and persistent resources.
